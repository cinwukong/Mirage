# What's new in Mirage

Mirage shows the notes for a new version once, after it updates. Each version is a "## <version>" heading followed by
"- " lines, newest first.

## 3.1.0
- A new look: calm dark purple, with gold only where it matters, clearer text and new fonts.
- Home has a big status pill with the microphone button next to it, a small orb that only moves while I listen, think or speak, suggestions, and a "Quiet for an hour" button.
- Five places in the menu: Home, Memory, Skills, Shortcuts and Settings. The new Skills page lets you switch what I can do on and off, with each one's settings.
- Settings are in four groups, with a search box. Each setting has a short name, and a "?" shows more about it.
- Making a shortcut is easier: press New shortcut, type or say the phrase, pick what it does, try it, then save it.
- Memory has three tabs: Things I remember, Past chats, and Calendar for your reminders and to-dos. Delete buttons are always visible.
- The corner hologram matches the new look, and its subtitles sit on a dark panel so they're easy to read on any background.

## 3.0.4
- Test results (the microphone test, testing the brain, update progress) now show in a box under the setting, instead of squeezing the setting's name into a narrow column.

## 3.0.3
- What I remember about you and your to-do list now live inside Mirage, on the Memory page, instead of in notes. I brought over what was already in your notes; the notes themselves are left as they were.
- Obsidian is no longer used: the notes-folder setting, "Open in Obsidian", the daily log notes and the link tools are gone. Your chat history still lets me look back at what we said.

## 3.0.2
- Subtitles now follow my voice sentence by sentence and scroll, so long replies are never cut off.
- Updates show a progress bar, and this note tells you what changed.
- A new Diagnostics page under Settings: test your microphone, see your graphics card, memory and my brain's status, and copy a report (without what you've said) when you need help.

## 3.0.1
- Safer: opening apps never goes through the command line, and I ask before typing into a terminal.
- Updates come only from published releases, are checked before they're installed, and roll back by themselves if the new version doesn't start.
- Downloads of my voice, hearing and brain are checked, so a broken download is never installed.
- The Claude Code "Log in" button works again.
